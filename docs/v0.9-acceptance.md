## Implementation checks

Structural comparison with the committed V0.8 baseline confirms that the existing scanner/context functions, KEV and EPSS functions, classifier, state loaders, remediation/verification derivation, and output writer are unchanged. The sorting expression and active-filter expression in `main()` are unchanged.

V0.9 adds only scan loading, evidence qualification, and their calls before verification derivation. A result is qualified when the supplied scan status is `succeeded` and its asset is explicitly covered. Qualification preserves the presence boolean and input structures.

## Expected failures and future pressure

The three retained tests in [test_known_v08_gaps.py](../tests/adversarial/test_known_v08_gaps.py) remain strict expected failures. None is an unexpected regression or grounds for adding excluded concepts to V0.9.

| Retained scenario | Result | Interpretation and remaining pressure |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Unqualified false evidence must not establish passed | XFAIL | This historical test directly calls `apply_verification_status()` with raw false evidence, bypassing qualification. That interpreter intentionally assumes supplied evidence is qualified. It does not demonstrate failure of the V0.9 `main()` boundary: failed-scan and uncovered-asset regressions pass. V0.9 checks declared success and coverage, but still has no timestamps/freshness or provenance establishing evidence trustworthiness. |
| Missing scanner observation must not remove previously open work | XFAIL | The open claim remains stored, but the finding disappears from queue visibility because only current scanner observations enter the pipeline. Distinguishing absence from incomplete observation and retaining prior work requires historical reconciliation; it is outside V0.9. |
| A previously passed finding reappearing should become active | XFAIL | Earlier resolved state and passing evidence can continue to suppress a later observation. Temporal association of evidence with observations, scan-run identities, history, and recurrence handling are outside V0.9. |

No timestamps, reconciliation, history, scan-run identities, or recurrence logic were introduced. The existing V0.8 validation document records that version's results; it is not a statement that all of its gaps remain unchanged in the V0.9 production path.
